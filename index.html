From farm to cup

How your coffee gets here

Read our story
01

Grown in shade

Our arabica grows under pepper vines and silver oak on family estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg, between 1,000 and 1,500 metres.

02

Aged by the monsoon

Green beans rest in open warehouses on the Malabar coast for 12 to 16 weeks, where wet winds swell them and soften their acidity.

03

Roasted every Monday

We roast in small batches each week, so the coffee in your cup is never more than seven days from the roaster.

Frequently asked

Quick answers

My order is late. What should I do?

Most orders are ready in 10 minutes. If yours takes longer than 20, show your order number at the counter and we'll make it a priority, with a free cookie on us.

How do I use my beans?

Once you have 150 beans, tell the barista you'd like to redeem a free drink. Any drink, any size.

When does the Weekly Regular discount reset?

Every Monday at midnight. Your 4th order between Monday and Sunday, and every order after it that week, gets 20% off.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes, within 2 minutes of placing it. Call us or tell the store, and we'll change or cancel it.

Do you have options for allergies?

We have oat, almond and coconut milk at no extra cost. Ask the barista for the full ingredient list of any item.

Do gift cards expire?

No. Monsoon Bean gift cards never expire and work in every store.
